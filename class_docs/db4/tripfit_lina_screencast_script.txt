TRIPFIT SCREENCAST SCRIPT - LINA KEY PATH SCENARIO
Target duration: approximately 4 minutes 30 seconds

Before recording:
- Begin on the TripFit landing screen.
- Follow the Group Vibe Check path.
- Let scanning, collection, sorting, and voting animations play briefly.
- Do not scroll through every option or read every line on screen.


0:00 - 0:20 | LANDING SCREEN

Action:
Show the TripFit landing screen and point briefly to Group Vibe Check.

Narration:
Hello everyone. Today I will show you TripFit, a mobile decision-support
app for travelers who have many saved ideas but struggle to choose what
to do next.

TripFit is not designed to generate more recommendations. Its goal is
to turn existing ideas into fewer options, clearer reasons, and a faster
decision.


0:20 - 0:40 | START GROUP VIBE CHECK

Action:
Click "Start a shared decision" under Group Vibe Check.

Narration:
For this scenario, imagine Lina. She is travelling in Barcelona with
three friends. They have a few hours before dinner, but their possible
plans are scattered across TikTok links, Google Maps pins, and group
messages.

Lina wants to help the group agree on one realistic plan without spending
their evening debating.


0:40 - 0:55 | CREATE SHARED TRIP

Action:
On the create group screen, click "Create group."

Narration:
Lina first creates a shared Barcelona Trip space. This gives everyone
one place to collect their ideas instead of keeping them fragmented
across different apps.


0:55 - 1:15 | FIND AND INVITE FRIENDS

Action:
Let the nearby-friends scanning animation appear. When Emma, Sofia, and
Elena appear, select all three and click "Invite friends."

Narration:
TripFit scans for nearby friends who are part of the trip. Lina invites
her three travel companions so that they can each contribute places they
already saved.


1:15 - 1:30 | COLLECT SHARED IDEAS

Action:
Let the incoming contributions appear, then click "Open shared idea pool."

Narration:
The group contributions arrive in the shared pool: TikTok links, chat
recommendations, and Google Maps pins.

In this prototype, this activity is simulated so that we can see the
collaborative process without switching between several user accounts.


1:30 - 1:48 | UNSORTED IDEA POOL

Action:
Briefly show the Barcelona idea pool, then click "Start Group Vibe Check."

Narration:
Lina can now see the real decision problem. The group already has many
ideas, but they are still unsorted.

Instead of searching for even more places, Lina starts a Group Vibe Check
to decide what actually fits the group right now.


1:48 - 2:12 | SET THE GROUP VIBE

Action:
Show the setup controls. Open one information icon briefly, for example
Budget or Group Energy, close it, then click "Confirm Vibe."

Narration:
Here, Lina expresses the current situation using constrained controls:
group energy, budget, environment, time left, and location.

She does not type a vague prompt. Each input directly maps to a practical
decision factor, and the information buttons explain what each parameter
means.


2:12 - 2:28 | SOURCES

Action:
Show the sources screen and click "Build group decision board."

Narration:
TripFit then makes its information sources visible. It uses the group's
saved places, Google Maps pins, and nearby realistic options.

This makes the result more transparent because Lina can understand where
the final choices come from.


2:28 - 2:42 | SORTING FEEDBACK

Action:
Wait for the sorting checklist to complete and advance automatically.

Narration:
During sorting, the app visibly explains what it is doing: combining
saved ideas, removing options that do not fit the constraints, considering
weather and budget, and prioritizing realistic group choices.


2:42 - 3:03 | DECISION BOARD

Action:
On the decision board, point to the filtered-out message and the marked
top option. Then click "Compare all options."

Narration:
The result is a decision board with only three realistic options.

Cerveceria Catalana is clearly marked as the best fit because it balances
time, budget, weather protection, and suitability for the group.

TripFit also explains that other options were filtered out, so this is not
a black-box recommendation.


3:03 - 3:20 | COMPARE TRADE-OFFS

Action:
Briefly show the comparison bottom sheet, then click the top option.

Narration:
If Lina wants more confidence, she can compare trade-offs without leaving
the decision board.

For example, Hidden Speakeasy is an appealing indoor alternative, but it
is less reliable for a group of four because a reservation may be needed.


3:20 - 3:36 | OPTION DETAIL

Action:
Show the Cerveceria Catalana image and explanation, then click "Choose this."

Narration:
Lina opens the top choice to understand why it fits now. TripFit shows the
venue, practical facts, its fit breakdown, and its main trade-off: it may
be crowded, so keeping a backup is sensible.


3:36 - 3:57 | QUICK GROUP CHECK

Action:
Let the voting indicators resolve, then click "Save backup and confirm."

Narration:
Because this is a group decision, TripFit runs a quick group check.
The organizer's choice appears immediately while the friends respond.

One person raises the concern that the restaurant could be crowded, so
TripFit suggests saving an alternative before final confirmation.


3:57 - 4:14 | BACKUP OPTION

Action:
Show the backup explanation and click "Save backup."

Narration:
The suggested backup is Hidden Speakeasy in El Born. It is not presented
as better than the main choice. It is a nearby indoor alternative with a
calmer drinks-focused atmosphere if the tapas venue is crowded, while
availability should still be checked first.


4:14 - 4:30 | CONFIRMATION

Action:
Show the confirmation screen, then click "Get directions."

Narration:
The confirmation screen provides clear task closure: "Decision made" and
"Good choice."

Lina can see the chosen destination and that a backup is ready. The group
has moved from scattered ideas to one agreed action.


4:30 - 4:48 | DIRECTIONS AND RECOVERY

Action:
On the directions screen, click "Use backup instead" once. You may toggle
back to the best choice if time permits.

Narration:
Finally, TripFit supports uncertainty in real travel situations. If the
main venue becomes too crowded, Lina can switch directly to the backup
route without restarting the whole decision process.


4:48 - 5:00 | CONCLUSION

Action:
Leave the final route screen visible.

Narration:
In summary, TripFit helps Lina's group transform scattered saved ideas
into one transparent, justified, and recoverable decision.

Its goal is not more recommendations, but fewer options, clearer reasons,
and faster group agreement. Thank you.


OPTIONAL ONE-SENTENCE MENTION OF THE SECOND KPS

If time remains, add this before "Thank you":

The prototype also includes a shorter Quick Low-Effort Nearby path for
a two-person, time-constrained decision before a train.
